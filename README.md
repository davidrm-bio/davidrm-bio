## David Rodriguez Morales

[Publications (ORCID)](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1819-6991) |
[Linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/david-rodriguez-morales)

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Bioinformatics at [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://www.uni-frankfurt.de/en) in the 
[Institute of Cardiovascular Regeneration](https://www.cardiovascular-regeneration.com/), where I study the molecular 
mechanisms that drive cardiac aging. 
My work integrates multi-omics technologies, including single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, to uncover pathways 
involved in age-associated decline and identify potential therapeutic targets.

I hold a B.Sc. in Biochemistry from the University of Granada and an M.Sc. in Bioinformatics from the University of Copenhagen. 
I enjoy coding in Python and R, building reproducible workflows, and experimenting with new tools and methods to better 
understand complex biological data.

Outside the lab, I enjoy running and spending time with my family and friends.
